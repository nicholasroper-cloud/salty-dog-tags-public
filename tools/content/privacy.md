# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 16, 2026**

Salty Dog Tags is one person, one laser and seven English bulldogs in Lehi, Utah. Short version: we collect what we need to make and ship your tag, we don't sell it, and we don't run ad trackers.

## What we collect {#collect}

**When you order**

- Name, email, and phone (optional).
- Your shipping address.
- What you want engraved (usually your dog's name and your number; that's the point), your tag choices, and notes for the engraver.
- For photo tags, the photo you upload.
- Any Salty Maze upgrade code you use.
- A scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, saved with the order to help spot fraud or one source flooding the form. We do not store your actual IP address or your browser type with the order.

**Your card: we never see it.** You enter it on Clover's secure hosted checkout page. It goes to Clover and never touches our servers.

**"Roast my dog"**

- Your dog's name, any notes, the tone you pick, and the photo if you add one.
- We do **not** keep the photo. It's used once to write the roast, then dropped.
- We keep the dog's name, tone and the roast for 90 days, plus any thumbs up/down or note you leave. Short snippets of liked and disliked lines stay longer, with no expiry, to teach the roaster what's funny. A snippet can include a dog's name.

**Salty Maze**: your three initials and score go on that day's public leaderboard. If you win an upgrade code, we store it with a random device ID. No name, no email.

**Kept in your browser, not by us**: your cart, your 18+ confirmation, your last order number and email (for the thank-you page), and maze data (device ID, best score, initials, plays, sound). Clear your browser's site data and it's gone.

**Rate limits**: to stop bots, we count AI requests by a scrambled (hashed) IP, never the IP itself. The count expires within an hour.

**Analytics**: Cloudflare shows us basic traffic (pages, rough location, browser). No ad trackers or pixels. Cloudflare may set security cookies to block bots.

**Emails and tags**: anything you send or tag us in.

## Why we use it {#why}

To make, proof and ship your tag; take payment and charge Utah sales tax when it applies; keep the Bulldog Promise; run the roast and the maze; stop fraud and abuse; and keep records the law requires. We don't sell your personal information or share it for targeted ads.

## Who we share it with {#share}

- **Clover (Fiserv)**: payments. Gets your name, email, phone, items and tag text. Your card details go straight from you to Clover, under Clover's own privacy policy.
- **Cloudflare**: hosts the site, stores orders and photo-tag photos, and runs Workers AI, one of the roast's AI services.
- **Anthropic**: the main AI behind the roast. Gets the photo, name and notes. We don't keep the photo; Anthropic may hold it briefly under its own terms (by default up to 30 days, longer if flagged for a safety review).
- **USPS**: your name and address, to deliver.
- **Order alerts**: a short summary (name, total, tag text) pings the owner's phone through Pushover and/or Twilio.
- **Email**: our email provider carries messages you send us.
- Anyone the law requires, or when someone's safety is at risk. If the business is sold, records move under this same policy.

## How long we keep it {#keep}

- Orders: until you ask us to delete them, except records we must keep for taxes.
- Uploaded photos and artwork (tags and ornaments): deleted within 120 days of your order; declined uploads within 7 days. We keep your rights confirmation, its time, and a file fingerprint for 3 years. If you ship a gift, we use the recipient's name and address only to deliver it.
- Roast photos: not kept. Roast text and feedback: 90 days. Liked and disliked line snippets: no expiry.
- Leaderboard: about a week. Upgrade codes: 31 days. Rate-limit counts: 1 hour.
- Reposts and Wall of Shame posts: until you ask us to take them down.

## Your choices {#choices}

- Email us to see, fix or delete your info. We'll answer within 30 days. Some order records have to stay for taxes.
- Skip the photo on the roast. It still works from the name and notes.
- Tell us not to repost your dog, or ask us to take a post down anytime.
- Clear your browser's site data to wipe the cart, 18+ setting and maze data.
- We don't send marketing email.

## Kids {#kids}

This site is for dog owners. It isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. **Please don't upload photos of people, and especially kids, to "Roast my dog."** The roaster turns down photos that mainly show a person, but it's a robot, so keep kids out of the frame. If you think a child under 13 sent us information, email us and we'll delete it.

## Security, changes, contact {#contact}

Orders and photos sit behind a key only the owner holds. Nothing online is perfectly safe, so we keep what we collect small. If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
