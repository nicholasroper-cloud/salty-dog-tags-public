## Licenses are a separate tag {#licenses}

Your dog's license tag comes from your city or county. The ID tag with your number comes from us. It's an ID tag, not a license or rabies tag. We wrote down the official rules for [Utah dog licenses in Lehi and Salt Lake County](/guides/utah-dog-license-and-tag-rules/) so you don't have to hunt for them. Down in Washington County, see [dog tags for St. George](/st-george-dog-tags/) and the [St. George license rules](/guides/st-george-dog-license-rules/).
