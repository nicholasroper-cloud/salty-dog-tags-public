## Where St. George dogs run {#dog-parks}

The City of St. George lists three fenced dog parks, and the neighbors have their own. Our [dog parks and trails guide](/guides/st-george-dog-parks-and-trails/) has the addresses and the rules from each city, state and park page:

- [St. George: Firehouse, J.C. Snow and Riverbend parks](/guides/st-george-dog-parks-and-trails/#st-george)
- [Washington City: Dog Town Dog Park](/guides/st-george-dog-parks-and-trails/#washington-city)
- [Hurricane: the city dog park](/guides/st-george-dog-parks-and-trails/#hurricane)
- [Snow Canyon State Park: every trail, on a 6-foot leash](/guides/st-george-dog-parks-and-trails/#snow-canyon)
- [Red Cliffs Desert Reserve: leash on, stay on the trail](/guides/st-george-dog-parks-and-trails/#red-cliffs)
- [Zion: the Pa'rus Trail only](/guides/st-george-dog-parks-and-trails/#zion)

Bring a leash even to the dog park. Every set of rules on that list wants it on at the gate.

## Around Washington County {#washington-county}

Rules change at the city line, so we keep each city in its own section of the guides instead of pretending one set fits all:

- **Washington City** licenses dogs for two years ($20 altered, $40 unaltered on the city's fee schedule) and wants dogs on a 6-foot leash in city parks. See [Washington City license rules](/guides/st-george-dog-license-rules/#washington-city) and [Dog Town Dog Park](/guides/st-george-dog-parks-and-trails/#washington-city).
- **Hurricane** wants a license by 4 months and the license and rabies tag on the collar. See [Hurricane license rules](/guides/st-george-dog-license-rules/#hurricane).
- **Ivins and Santa Clara** share one public safety department, with an animal control office in Ivins. An Ivins license lasts until the rabies certificate expires. See [Ivins and Santa Clara](/guides/st-george-dog-license-rules/#ivins-and-santa-clara).

Elsewhere in Utah? The [Utah dog tags page](/utah-dog-tags/) covers the Wasatch Front.
