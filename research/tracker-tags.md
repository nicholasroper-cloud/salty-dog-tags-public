# Engravable AirTag Dog-Tag Holders: Shortlist, Wholesale, and Pricing
Researched 2026-10-11 by Doc. Owner's ask: "can you find a tracker ones we can engrave on." Laser is a 60 W MOPA fiber (see tag-blanks.md section 7), so bare stainless, anodized aluminum, brass, and titanium all mark directly; plastic and silicone do not.

Method note: Amazon blocks page scraping for most of each listing. Titles, brand, material field, ratings, and prices were read from the parts that rendered (variant tables, details tables, and "frequently bought together" widgets). Anything marked "est." or "unverified" was not visible and needs a 30-second check in a browser before ordering. Every ASIN below resolves.

## Bottom line
- The category you want (a metal-faced holder that sits on the collar and takes an AirTag) exists, but it is thin: one OEM design family with a stainless steel front cover (sold as PALAMEDE, ONEONTA, Taghelp, and others), one solid-stainless slide-on sleeve (SANKALA), and one thin anodized-aluminum clip (BuddyArmor). Everything else "metal" on Amazon is a keychain case, a metal loop on a plastic body, or silicone.
- Buy first: the PALAMEDE stainless-cover holder (B0DK24M3BV, $13.99, 4.5 stars, 97 ratings, IP68, stainless steel listed as the material). The cover is a flat disc roughly the size of the AirTag, it comes off with screws, so you can lay it flat on the laser bed and mark it with no AirTag inside and no fixture problem. That is the only design here where engraving-before-assembly is possible.
- Nobody sells these as "engravable blanks." No reviewer on any listing mentioned laser engraving. BuddyArmor is the one vendor that engraves its own aluminum holder (25 characters, one line), which proves that face takes a laser but also shows how little room there is.
- No metal engravable holder exists for Samsung SmartTag2, Tile, Fi, Tractive, or Whistle. Whistle shut down on 2025-08-31. The nearest adjacent SKU is a rivet-on brass or stainless nameplate for Fi-ready collars, which you could laser as a "Fi plate" product.
- Suggested retail for "Tracker Tag + engraving" (AirTag not included): $29.99 standard mark, $34.99 MOPA color mark, $54.99 bundled with an AirTag 2nd gen. Detail in the pricing section.

## 3. Samsung SmartTag2, Tile, and GPS collars
- Samsung SmartTag2: every holder found on Amazon is silicone (2-, 3-, and 4-packs, B0CLV986CR, B0DM1Y1YKQ, B0DXF84QBP, B0CLVC7RV6) or ABS with adhesive. GoTags sells a personalized SmartTag2 holder, material not stated, almost certainly silicone like their AirTag line. Zee.Dog Jelly Galaxy Tag Holder is silicone at $8.99. No metal engravable holder exists. Unknown, not just unfound: searched Amazon, Walmart, and general web.
- Tile: Tile Pro Holder is a flexible plastic sleeve. QALO TraQ (Tile Mate inside) is ABS with a silicone skin, personalized by print, not engraving. No metal option.
- Fi: Fi sells no engraved plate. Third parties do: dogIDs "Fi Series 2 Compatible Nameplate Collar" $39 to $44 (riveted stainless or brass nameplate, Series 2 only), Mimi Green Fi Series 3 nylon collar with engraved buckle on Chewy (one reviewer says the buckle engraving wore illegible), Stunt Puppy "Fi-ready" collars (stitched, not engraved). dogIDs also sells rivet-on nameplates in stainless and solid brass, up to 3 lines of 15 characters. Opportunity: a laser-marked rivet-on brass plate is a "Fi plate" SKU you could offer without any tracker-specific tooling.
- Tractive: sells rubber mounting clips only ($6.99 to $9.99, out of stock), no tag or plate.
- Whistle: platform shut down 2025-08-31 after Tractive acquired it; devices are bricked. Do not build anything for Whistle.

## 6. Laser and build notes specific to these parts
- Engrave the PALAMEDE cover off the body. Four screws out, cover flat on the bed, mark, reassemble. No AirTag heat exposure, no focus problem from the dome, no risk to the silicone seal.
- Thin stainless disc (est. 0.5 to 0.8 mm): use a surface anneal or black mark, not a deep engrave. A deep pass can warp the disc and break the seal. MOPA color marking is an anneal, so it is the right process here and it is the differentiator nobody else in the category has.
- Black or pink covers: run a strip test. PVD or paint strips to silver (high contrast). Black oxide needs a white mark. One tag per color.
- SANKALA sleeve: it cannot be disassembled, so mark it assembled, empty. Check the front for curvature; if domed, keep text inside the center 20 mm and set focus at the center.
- Hybrid fallback if none of the metal faces mark well: Elevation Lab TagVault Pet (IP69, $12 to $16) on the collar plus one of your existing stainless bone blanks on a ring. Sell it as "Tracker Set" at the same $29.99. Less elegant, zero technical risk.
- Two-line rule: a 28 mm usable circle at 4 mm cap height gives roughly 12 characters per line, two lines, with a third short line possible. Most of the current sayings fit if abbreviated; the long ones ("I'm lost, call my mom, she's freaking out") need the bone blank, not the tracker tag.
