## Lehi: Willow Park and Exchange Park

- Lehi has **two dedicated dog parks: Willow Park and Exchange Park**. Every other city park requires a leash.
- **No dogs at all** at Lehi Sports Park or Veterans Memorial Ball Park.
- Most Lehi parks are open 5 AM to 11 PM, and parks can't be occupied 11 PM to 5 AM.
- Willow Park's dog park sits next to the Willow Park campground, 2998 West 300 North. Willow Park itself is open 7 AM to 10 PM.

Sources: [Lehi parks FAQ](https://www.lehi-ut.gov/recreation-community/parks-trails/faqs), [Willow Park](https://www.lehi-ut.gov/recreation-community/parks-trails/parks/willow-park/).

## Saratoga Springs: Saratoga Ridge Park

- The dog park is at the south end of **Saratoga Ridge Park**, 532 N Mount Saratoga Blvd.
- The park is open 6 AM to 10 PM.
- The dog park is **closed the 2nd and 4th Tuesdays** of each month for maintenance.
- Water fountains, including the dog park's, are winterized October 15 to April 15. Bring water. Bulldogs especially.

Source: [Saratoga Ridge Park](https://www.saratogasprings-ut.gov/Facilities/Facility/Details/Saratoga-Ridge-Park-68).

## Orem: Timpanogos Park

Orem's animal control page names the dog park area at **Timpanogos Park** as the city park where dogs can run free. It doesn't list hours or rules, so call the city before your first visit.

Source: [Orem City Animal Control](https://orem.gov/animal-control/).

## Provo: Bicentennial Park

Provo's park guidelines name the designated **dog park inside Bicentennial Park** as the city's off-leash area. Check provo.gov for hours and current rules.

Source: [Provo Parks Use Guidelines (PDF)](https://www.provo.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1184/Parks-Use-Guidelines-PDF).

## Draper (Salt Lake County, but close enough)

- Designated off-leash areas: **Dayland Dog Park**, **Galena Dog Park** and the **West Bluff** off-leash test area at 1455 E. Deer Ridge Drive.
- Dogs are allowed **on leash** on the Bonneville Shoreline Trail and the trails below it.
- Dogs are **not allowed** in the Corner Canyon watershed area above the Bonneville Shoreline Trail.

Source: [Draper rules and trail etiquette](https://www.draperutah.gov/living-in-draper-city/parks-and-trails/rules-trail-etiquette/).

## Before you go

- Bring a leash even to the dog park. You'll need it in the parking lot.
- Bring water, bags and a dog who comes back when called. Two out of three is typical.
- Get your dog licensed (here are the [Utah license rules](https://saltydogtags.com/guides/utah-dog-license-and-tag-rules/)) and wearing an ID tag with your phone number.

**Where can my dog be off leash in Lehi?**

Lehi City lists two dedicated dog parks, Willow Park and Exchange Park. All other Lehi parks require dogs to be leashed, and dogs are not allowed at Lehi Sports Park or Veterans Memorial Ball Park.

**Is there a dog park in Saratoga Springs, Utah?**
