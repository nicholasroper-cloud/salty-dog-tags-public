## St. George

- Dogs **4 months or older** must be licensed by the city, and a dog you get during the year needs a license **within 10 days**.
- Fees per dog: **$10** if spayed or neutered, **$20** if not.
- Tags go on sale **January 1** and expire **December 31**. Renewals are due by **February 28**. After that the late fee is **$5 per month per dog**, up to $20 per dog.
- **Only two dogs per residence** are permitted in St. George, the city's licensing page says.
- Just visiting? Temporary-resident dogs with a current license tag from another city or county don't need a St. George license.
- Apply online or with the fillable PDF on the city page. The online form asks for **proof of rabies vaccination** and, if it applies, a spay or neuter certificate. Owners of unlicensed dogs can be cited and fined.
- **St. George Animal Shelter:** 605 E Waterworks Rd, 435-627-4350. By appointment only, Monday to Saturday; closed Sunday.

Sources: [City of St. George dog licensing](https://sgcityutah.gov/doglicense/) and [St. George Animal Services and Shelter](https://www.sgcityutah.gov/departments/police_department/animal_services___shelter.php).

## Washington City

- **All dogs** in the city must be licensed, and a license is **valid for two years**.
- You need **current proof of rabies** to license. Spayed or neutered pets get a discount.
- Part-time residents who are in the city **longer than 30 days** must license their dogs.
- The city's fee schedule lists a two-year license at **$20 altered** or **$40 not altered**, **$50** for a third dog, **$75** for a fourth dog, a **$120** lifetime license and **$5** for a replacement tag.
- If your dog is picked up wandering, the impound fee is **$50** for a first offense plus **$10 per day**.
- Apply online from the city page. **Washington City Animal Shelter:** 95 E Industrial Dr., (435) 673-7194. Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.; closed weekends and holidays.

Sources: [Washington City Animal Welfare Services](https://washingtonutah.gov/services/publicsafety/police/animalwelfare) and the [Washington City Master Fee Schedule](https://washingtonutah.gov/government/budget/MasterFeeSchedule/&key=7).

## Hurricane

- City ordinance: your dog must be licensed **by 4 months of age**, and a new dog **within 30 days** of getting it.
- Each residential address may license **up to four dogs** per calendar year.
- **A license and rabies tag must be attached to your dog's collar.** Hurricane is the one city here that says so on its page.
- Bring proof of a **current rabies vaccination**: a current certificate from your vet. Online, a photo of the rabies tag is not accepted.
- Fees: **$10** spayed or neutered, **$25** not. When we checked, the page said licenses were due January 1, 2026 and fees go up by $20 per residence from March 1, 2026. Check the page for next year's dates.
- Buy a license at the animal shelter, at the Police Department (90 S 700 W, cash or check), online, or at Desert Vet and Zion Vet between December and February.
- **Hurricane Animal Shelter:** 2084 S 700 W, 435-635-8314.

Source: [Hurricane City Animal Control and Shelter](https://www.cityofhurricane.com/159/Animal-Control-Shelter).

## Ivins and Santa Clara

Ivins and Santa Clara share the Santa Clara–Ivins Public Safety Department, which handles animal problems for both cities. Its **animal control office** is at 474 N 200 W, Ivins, 435-628-1049.

- **Ivins:** the city's dog license application lists **$5 per dog per year** for an altered dog and **$10** for an unaltered dog. A **current rabies certificate** is required, and the license is **valid until the rabies certificate expires** (you can pick a shorter term). The form lists Ivins City at 85 N. Main St., 435-628-0606.
- **Santa Clara:** we couldn't find Santa Clara's own license fees or rules on an official page, so we're not going to guess. Call Santa Clara–Ivins animal control.

Sources: [Ivins dog license application (IC Form 3002)](https://ivinsutah.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Dog-License-Application-IC-Form-3002-2024-05-10.pdf), [Ivins animal shelter and control](https://ivinsutah.gov/document-category/animal-shelter-control/) and [Santa Clara–Ivins Public Safety](https://ivinsutah.gov/public-safety-3/).

## New to Washington County?

The deadlines start the day the dog (or you) arrives:

- **St. George:** a dog you get during the year needs a license within 10 days. Visiting dogs with a current tag from elsewhere don't need one.
- **Washington City:** part-time residents who stay longer than 30 days must license their dogs.
- **Hurricane:** a new dog needs a license within 30 days.

## What goes on the collar

None of these pages dictate what your personal ID tag has to say. They issue the license tag, and the rest is up to you. Our advice, from a house where every bulldog has tested the fence at least once:

- **The license tag** from your city, so animal control can look your dog up.
- **The rabies tag**, if your vet gave you one. In Hurricane, the license and rabies tag are required on the collar.
- **An ID tag with your phone number**, big and readable. A license number means a call to an office during office hours. Your number means a call to you, right now.

**To be clear: our tags are ID tags, not license or rabies tags, and they don't satisfy any licensing requirement.** You still need the license from your city.

**How much is a dog license in St. George, Utah?**

The City of St. George charges $10 per dog if spayed or neutered and $20 if not. Tags go on sale January 1 and expire December 31. Renewals are due by February 28, with a $5 per month late fee per dog after that, up to $20.

**At what age does a dog need a license in St. George?**

Dogs 4 months or older must be licensed by the city, and a dog you get during the year has to be licensed within 10 days. The online form asks for proof of rabies vaccination.

**How many dogs can I have in St. George?**

The city's dog licensing page says only two dogs per residence are permitted in St. George. Hurricane allows up to four licensed dogs per residential address. Other cities set their own limits, so check yours.

**How long does a Washington City dog license last?**

Two years. Washington City requires all dogs in the city to be licensed, with current proof of rabies, and its fee schedule lists $20 for an altered dog and $40 for an unaltered dog for two years.
