## St. George city dog parks

The City of St. George lists three parks with a dog park:

- **Firehouse Park**, 1929 West 1800 North. A 4-acre neighborhood park, and the dog park is a secure fenced area for off-leash play. Drinking fountains, restrooms and parking.
- **J.C. Snow Park**, 275 East 900 South. A 12-acre community park with a dog park, restrooms, drinking fountains and the trailhead for the Hilton Dr. Trail.
- **Riverbend Park**, 810 W. Las Colinas. A fully fenced dog park, a restroom, a drinking fountain, parking and a connection to the trail system.

Each of those pages links the city's dog park rules brochure (the brochure itself is dated 2016). It says:

- Dogs must be **at least 4 months old, fully vaccinated and currently licensed**.
- Dogs must be **leashed when entering and leaving**. Inside, owners stay in the park, in view of their dogs, with the dogs under voice control.
- **No spike collars.** Aggressive dogs and female dogs in heat are not allowed in.
- No food or glass bottles. Scoop the poop, seal it in a bag, and fill any holes your dog digs.
- Children 15 and under must be actively supervised by an adult. **The park closes at 10 p.m.**

For the rest of the city's parks, the Parks page links the park ordinance (City Code Title 7, Chapter 3). The code site wouldn't load for us, so we're not quoting it. Read it before you unclip anywhere that isn't a fenced dog park.

## Washington City

- **Dog Town Dog Park**, 450 South 200 East. Off-leash within the fenced areas, with one area for dogs **under 25 pounds** and one for dogs **over 25 pounds**, plus a water feature for dogs to play in and cool off. Restrooms, a pavilion, a playground, benches and picnic sites.
- In Washington City's other parks, the posted park rules say **pets must be on a 6-foot leash** and the owner is responsible for waste. City parks are open **6 a.m. to 10 p.m.**

Sources: [Dog Town Dog Park](https://washingtonutah.gov/recreation/parks/DogTownDogPark) and [Washington City parks](https://washingtonutah.gov/recreation/parks).

## Hurricane

Hurricane City lists a dog park with **active and passive run areas**, a natural habitat area, parking, pavilions, benches and a perimeter fence. The city says the fencing is installed, the park is open to the public for **limited use**, and it's still taking donations to finish the park. The page gives no street address, so start from the city's facility page.

Source: [Hurricane City dog park](https://www.cityofhurricane.com/Facilities/Facility/Details/Dog-Park-4).

## Snow Canyon State Park

- **Dogs are permitted on all park trails and in the campground.** At all times they must be leashed (**6 feet max**), under their owner's control, on designated mapped trails, with all waste scooped, bagged and thrown away.
- Why so strict: Snow Canyon is part of the Red Cliffs Desert Reserve, a conservation area for Mojave desert tortoises. The park says tortoises can't tell a dog from a coyote, and that two types of rattlesnakes, Gila monsters, scorpions and spiders live in the canyon. The park's words: an off-leash dog has a much higher chance of coming nose to nose with a venomous animal.
- **Johnson Canyon is closed March 15 through September 14**, except for ranger-guided hikes.
- Summer: the park says daytime temperatures in July and August frequently top 105 degrees. Read our [desert heat guide](https://saltydogtags.com/guides/desert-heat-dog-safety-st-george/) before a summer hike.

Sources: [Snow Canyon State Park](https://stateparks.utah.gov/parks/snow-canyon/) (the questions section) and [current conditions](https://stateparks.utah.gov/parks/snow-canyon/current-conditions/).

## Red Cliffs Desert Reserve

- **All pets must be on a leash**, to prevent wildlife disturbance and to avoid conflicts with other people. Officers patrol the reserve and may issue citations.
- Desert tortoises are protected by federal law, and it's illegal for pets, like people, to harass or harm them. The reserve also warns that rattlesnakes and Gila monsters may bite a pet in self-defense.
- In the reserve's Lowland Zone, travel is restricted to designated trails.
- The one exception: hunting dogs may travel off-leash with a licensed hunter in the act of hunting during official hunting seasons.

Source: [Red Cliffs Desert Reserve rules and regulations](https://www.redcliffsdesertreserve.com/rules).

## Zion National Park

- **The only trail that allows pets is the Pa'rus Trail**, which starts at the Zion Canyon Visitor Center.
- Leashed pets are welcome along public roads and parking areas, in developed campgrounds and picnic areas, and on the Zion Lodge grounds. They are not allowed on any other trail, in wilderness areas, on shuttle buses or in public buildings.
- The leash can be no longer than **6 feet**, and retractable leashes that extend further than 6 feet are not allowed.
- **Keep dogs out of the water.** The park monitors the Virgin River for toxic cyanobacteria, and dogs may ingest cyanobacteria or untreated water.
- Don't leave a pet in the car; the park says Zion is hot and a vehicle can quickly warm to dangerous levels for most of the year. It lists boarding kennels in Hurricane, St. George, Kanab, Cedar City and Rockville.

Source: [Zion National Park: pets](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/pets.htm).

## FAQ

**Where are the dog parks in St. George?**

The city lists fenced dog parks at Firehouse Park (1929 West 1800 North), J.C. Snow Park (275 East 900 South) and Riverbend Park (810 W. Las Colinas). Washington City has Dog Town Dog Park at 450 South 200 East.

**Are dogs allowed in Snow Canyon State Park?**

Yes. Utah State Parks says dogs are permitted on all park trails and in the campground, on a leash 6 feet or shorter, under control, on designated mapped trails, with waste bagged and binned.

**Which trails in Zion allow dogs?**

Only the Pa'rus Trail, which starts at the Zion Canyon Visitor Center. Pets must be on a leash no longer than 6 feet and are not allowed on other trails, in wilderness areas, on shuttle buses or in public buildings.

**Do dogs have to be leashed in the Red Cliffs Desert Reserve?**

Yes. The reserve's rules say all pets must be on a leash, and officers patrol and may issue citations. The one exception is hunting dogs with a licensed hunter during official hunting seasons.

**What are the rules inside a St. George dog park?**

The rules brochure the city links from its dog park pages says dogs must be at least 4 months old, fully vaccinated and currently licensed, leashed when entering and leaving, and under voice control. The park closes at 10 p.m.
