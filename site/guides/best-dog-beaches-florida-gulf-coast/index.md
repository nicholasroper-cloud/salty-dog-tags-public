## Before You Go: The Unglamorous Stuff That Matters

Here's the boring part first, because it's the part that keeps your dog alive and your day fun.

- **Heat is the real boss.** Florida sun is no joke, and flat-faced breeds like bulldogs, pugs, and Frenchies have a harder time cooling down. Go early in the morning or late in the afternoon. Skip midday in summer entirely.
- **Hot sand burns paws.** If it's too hot for the back of your hand, it's too hot for your dog's feet.
- **Bring fresh water.** Your dog will try to drink the Gulf. The Gulf will win. Pack a bowl and more water than seems reasonable.
- **Life vests aren't optional for non-swimmers.** Some breeds sink like a bag of sand. Ours qualify. A dog life jacket with a handle on top is a lifesaver, literally.
- **Rinse off after.** Salt and sand irritate skin, paws, and those deep face wrinkles. A quick freshwater rinse helps.
- **Watch for local hazards.** Red tide, jellyfish, stingrays, and washed-up fish are all part of Gulf life at different times of year. Check local conditions before you go.
- **Make sure the tag is current.** Off-leash beaches are exactly where a dog decides to take a self-guided tour. A tag with a working phone number is the fastest way home.

## Pensacola Beach Dog Beaches (Santa Rosa Island)

Way up in the Panhandle, Pensacola Beach has two designated pet-friendly beaches on Santa Rosa Island, one at each end of the island. It's the classic Panhandle look: bright white sand, emerald-green water, and enough space for your dog to feel like the main character.

**Good to know:**

- The west one is by the Fort Pickens Road lot (21E, also called 21.5). The east one is by the Via de Luna lot (28B, also called 28.5). All other island beaches are closed to dogs, Casino Beach included. Dogs outside the marked zones can get you a conversation with someone in uniform.
- Leashes are required at all times.
- Hours run sunrise to sunset, and 7 a.m. to sunset during turtle season (May 1 to Oct 31).
- Bring your own shade. The Panhandle sun bounces off that white sand, and you'll feel it.

Check Escambia County and Santa Rosa Island Authority info for current locations and rules before you head over the bridge ([visitpensacolabeach.com](https://visitpensacolabeach.com/things-to-do-dog-beach/)).

## Pier Park Dog Beach (Panama City Beach)

Panama City Beach has a designated dog beach near Pier Park, which makes it an easy add-on to a PCB trip. You can get your beach time in, then go deal with the rest of humanity at the shops nearby.

**Good to know:**

- It's a designated stretch, so stay inside the posted boundaries.
- A leash is required, and your dog must stay under your command. Dogs need to be current on shots and **wear ID tags**, which is exactly the kind of rule we like.
- Max 3 dogs per person, no dogs under 4 months, no females in season, and a muzzle for dogs with a bite history. Fines start at $50 outside the boundaries.
- The beach is west of the Russell-Fields City Pier, in front of Pier Park. Confirm the current setup with the [city's dog page](https://pcbfl.gov/about-us/visitors/dogs) before you go.
- PCB gets crowded in peak season. If your dog is the "Small dog. Big mouth." type, a quieter weekday morning might save your nerves.

## Honeymoon Island State Park Pet Beach (Dunedin)

Honeymoon Island, just off Dunedin in Pinellas County, is one of the best-known dog beaches in the Tampa Bay area. The state park has a designated pet beach where leashed dogs can enjoy the Gulf. Your dog gets a real coastline, and you get a real nature-park feel instead of high-rises.

**Good to know:**

- Dogs need to be leashed. Florida State Parks generally require a leash no longer than six feet, so check the park's current pet policy.
- There's a park entrance fee, so plan for it.
- Pets are allowed on the designated pet beach, not on every beach in the park. Follow the signs.
- Shade can be limited, so an umbrella or pop-up shelter is worth bringing.
- Storm caveat: Hurricanes Helene and Milton closed the park in 2024. It partly reopened in late November 2024 with the pet beach open, though some facilities stayed closed into 2025. Check the [Florida State Parks page](https://www.floridastateparks.org/learn/activities-along-shore-honeymoon) for current status and the entrance fee.

Honeymoon Island is a great pick for dogs who like sniffing more than sprinting. That's basically every bulldog ever. They're not swimming. They're conducting an investigation.

## Fort De Soto Park Dog Beach & Paw Playground (Tierra Verde)

If Gulf Coast dog beaches had a headliner, it would be Fort De Soto. This Pinellas County park near Tierra Verde has a designated dog beach where dogs can be off-leash, plus the **Paw Playground**, a fenced dog park with separate areas for large and small dogs.

Off-leash beach time is the dream for a lot of dogs, and this is one of the places that actually allows it.

**Good to know:**

- The off-leash rules apply to the designated dog beach and Paw Playground only. Everywhere else in the park, follow the posted leash rules.
- Fort De Soto is popular. Weekends can get busy, so arrive early if you want parking and elbow room.
- Off-leash means your dog has choices. Some dogs make bad ones. If yours treats recall as a rumor, keep a long line handy or stick to the fenced Paw Playground.
- Rinse stations and water access can vary, so bring your own anyway.
- Storm caveat: the park closed after Hurricanes Helene and Milton in September 2024. The dog beach reopened January 6, 2025, and the county warned some areas might stay closed. Check [Pinellas County](https://pinellas.gov/news/fort-de-soto-park-fort-dog-beach-fishing-piers-reopen/) for current status.

This is the beach where a dog in a "Lifeguard (off duty)" tag fits right in. Supervising from a towel counts as working, right?

## Brohard Paw Park (Venice)

Venice calls itself the Shark Tooth Capital of the World, and Brohard Paw Park lets your dog join the hunt. It's a dog beach right on the Gulf in Sarasota County where dogs can enjoy the sand and water.

**Good to know:**

- Brohard Paw Park has had off-leash beach access, but confirm the current rules with Sarasota County before you go.
- The park has fenced small and large dog areas, plus dog showers and fountains, which helps with the rinse tip.
- Shark tooth hunting and a dog that eats everything can be a fun mix. Keep an eye out.
- Venice gets steady traffic year-round, especially in season, so earlier is better.

If your dog has ever eaten homework and found it delicious, keep an eye on what goes in that mouth here. Fossils aren't snacks, even if your dog strongly disagrees.

## Lee County Dog Beach (Between Fort Myers Beach and Lovers Key)

Down in Southwest Florida, Lee County runs an off-leash dog beach near the south end of Estero Island, close to Lovers Key. It's a tidal flat, so check the tide. It's known for shallow, calm water, which is great news for dogs whose swimming style is mostly "panic."

**Good to know:**

- This area was hit by Hurricane Ian in 2022 and again in 2024, and it reopened with limited amenities in October 2024. There's a portable toilet only, no restrooms, and a max of 2 dogs per person. Check Lee County's official information before you drive down.
- Calm, shallow water is ideal for wading. Even so, non-swimmers should wear a life jacket.
- Parking fees apply.

If you're traveling from Naples or Fort Myers, this is one of the most popular dog-friendly beach options in the area. Just make sure it's open before you promise your dog a beach day.

## A Few Honorable Rules of Thumb for Gulf Coast Dog Beaches

Not every beach on this list fits every dog. Here's a quick way to choose:

- **Off-leash lovers with solid recall:** Fort De Soto, Brohard Paw Park, Lee County Dog Beach (rules permitting).
- **Leashed sniffers and nature walkers:** Honeymoon Island.
- **Panhandle road trippers:** Pensacola Beach and Pier Park Dog Beach.
- **Bulldogs, pugs, and other flat-faced legends:** Whatever's closest, earliest, and shadiest. Keep it short. Leave before they hit their limit. They'll nap on the ride home with the confidence of a dog who did something important.

## FAQ

Still stuck? [hello@saltydogtags.com](mailto:hello@saltydogtags.com). A human answers. The dogs supervise.

**Are dogs allowed on all Florida Gulf Coast beaches?**

No. Many Gulf Coast beaches don't allow dogs, and the ones that do usually limit them to designated areas. Always check the official city, county, or state park website for current rules before you go.

**Which Gulf Coast dog beaches allow dogs off-leash?**

Fort De Soto Park's dog beach and Paw Playground are well-known off-leash spots. Brohard Paw Park in Venice and the Lee County Dog Beach near Lovers Key have also allowed off-leash dogs. Rules can change, so confirm before you unclip.

**Is Honeymoon Island dog-friendly?**

Yes. Honeymoon Island State Park has a designated pet beach, and dogs must be on a 6-ft leash. A park entrance fee applies. Check the Florida State Parks website for the latest pet policy.

**Can bulldogs and other flat-faced breeds go to the beach?**

They can, with care. Flat-faced breeds overheat easily, so go during cooler hours, bring shade and plenty of fresh water, and keep visits short. If your dog can't swim, use a life jacket. Talk to your vet if you're unsure what your dog can handle.

**What should I put on my dog's beach tag?**

At minimum, a phone number you'll actually answer. Many owners also add the dog's name and a note like "microchipped" or a medical alert. Room for a joke is optional, but recommended.
