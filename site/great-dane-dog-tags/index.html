Built for a dog that hits things at tail height

A Great Dane's tag lives at counter level, which means it has seen things. It gets dragged across the kitchen counter mid-heist, dunked in a water bowl the size of a kiddie pool, and swung into every door frame in the house. Drool does the rest.

Every tag is laser engraved, so the words are cut into the metal instead of painted on top. There's no ink to chip, peel or wash off.

Shake your head and redecorate the walls. The tag can take it.

Which shape fits your Great Dane

Tags here come in one size per shape, so the shape you pick sets the size. A Great Dane has a big head and a thick collar, and a small tag disappears against all that. Go with the military, about 2 inches, or the polished stainless bone, the biggest we make at 48 mm, with a paw cut-out. Both are easy to read at a glance and easy to find under the fur. The bone, heart and paw are Medium, about 1.5 inches. They work if you like a quieter tag, or if the collar is slim. Leave the round for the lapdogs. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.

Great Dane tag FAQ

Still stuck? hello@saltydogtags.com. A human answers. The dogs supervise.

Which tag shape fits a Great Dane?

Tags come in one size per shape, so the shape sets the size. A Great Dane has a big head and a thick collar, so go with the military (about 2 inches) or the polished stainless bone (48 mm, with a paw cut-out). The bone, heart and paw (about 1.5 inches) suit a slimmer collar. Leave the round for the lapdogs.

Will drool, water and humidity ruin an engraved tag?

No. The words are laser engraved into the metal, not printed on top, so there's nothing to wash off, peel or fade.

Can I put my Great Dane's name and my phone number on the tag?

Yes, that's the standard build. The funny saying goes on the front, and your dog's name and your phone number are engraved on the back.

How much does a Great Dane tag cost?

Tags start at $12. Your Dane will cost considerably more, but that's between you and your grocery bill.

What happens if the engraving comes out wrong?

If we mis-engrave it, we remake it free. Tags are engraved and shipped from Lehi, Utah in 1 to 2 business days, then USPS takes 3 to 5 days in transit. US only.
