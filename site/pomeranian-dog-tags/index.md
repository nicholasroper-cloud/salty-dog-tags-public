## Fluff eats tags. Engraving doesn't care.

A Pomeranian doesn't wear a tag so much as absorb it. The coat swallows it, the brushing drags across it, and a humid morning turns the whole dog into a damp cotton ball. Printed tags rub off under all that.

We cut the words into the metal instead of printing them on top. Nothing is painted on, so nothing peels off.

Survives the third pool-deck zoomie. And the fourth.

## Which shape fits your Pomeranian

Tags here come in one size per shape, so the shape you pick is the size you get. Small collars and small necks want small tags. For a Pomeranian, start with the round, about 1.2 inches, or the heart, about 1.5 inches. Both sit flat and stay out of the way of a tiny chin. The bone and paw are also about 1.5 inches if you want the classic look. Skip the military, which is about 2 inches, and the polished bone, the biggest tag we make at 48 mm. They'll look like a dinner plate on a Pomeranian. Every shape gets the same two-sided engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back. Pick the shape. The size comes with it.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.

**Which tag shape fits a Pomeranian?**

Tags come in one size per shape, so the shape sets the size. For a Pomeranian, start with the round (about 1.2 inches) or the heart (about 1.5 inches). Both sit flat under a small chin. The military (about 2 inches) and the polished stainless bone (48 mm) are too big for a dog this size.

**Will all that fur wear down the engraving?**

No. The words are laser engraved into the metal, not printed on top, so brushing and constant fluff contact don't wipe them off.

**Can I put my Pom's name and my phone number on the tag?**

Yes, that's the standard build. The funny saying goes on the front, and your dog's name and your phone number are engraved on the back.
