## Which shape fits your Aussie

Tags here come in one size per shape, so picking a shape is picking a size. For an Aussie, the sweet spot is the bone, heart or paw. All three are Medium, about 1.5 inches, which suits a standard collar and a medium neck without swallowing it. Want it a little bigger? The military runs about 2 inches and gives long sayings room to breathe. Want it smaller? The round is about 1.2 inches. The polished stainless bone is the biggest we make at 48 mm, and it comes with a paw cut-out, which is handy if your Aussie also likes to be noticed. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.

## Aussie tag FAQ

Still stuck? [hello@saltydogtags.com](mailto:hello@saltydogtags.com). A human answers. The dogs supervise.

**Which tag shape fits an Aussie?**

Tags come in one size per shape, so picking a shape is picking a size. For an Aussie, the bone, heart or paw (all Medium, about 1.5 inches) is the sweet spot. Want it bigger? The military is about 2 inches. Want it smaller? The round is about 1.2 inches.

**Will mud, drool and rough play wear off the engraving?**

No. The text is laser engraved into the metal, so scuffing doesn't erase it.

**Can I put my Aussie's name and my phone number on the tag?**

Yes. Saying on the front, name and phone number on the back.

**Do you have sayings written specifically for Aussies?**

12 on this page. Each fits the tag as written.

**What should I put on an Australian Shepherd's tag?**

One joke for the front, two short lines at most, like Herds kids. Unpaid. Unstoppable. Put your dog's name and a phone number on the back. Aussies are escape artists, so the number matters more than the joke.

**What happens if the engraving comes out wrong?**

If we mis-engrave it, we remake it free. Tags are engraved and shipped from Lehi, Utah in 1 to 2 business days, then USPS takes 3 to 5 days in transit. US only.
