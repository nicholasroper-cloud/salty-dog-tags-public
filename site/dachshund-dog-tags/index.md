## Which shape fits your Dachshund

Tags here come in one size per shape, so picking a shape is picking a size. For a Dachshund, the sweet spot is the bone, heart or paw. All three are Medium, about 1.5 inches, which suits a standard collar and a medium neck without swallowing it. Want it a little bigger? The military runs about 2 inches and gives long sayings room to breathe. Want it smaller? The round is about 1.2 inches. The polished stainless bone is the biggest we make at 48 mm, and it comes with a paw cut-out, which is handy if your Dachshund also likes to be noticed. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.
