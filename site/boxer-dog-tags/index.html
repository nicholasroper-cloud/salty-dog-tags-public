Which shape fits your Boxer

Tags here come in one size per shape, so picking a shape is picking a size. For a Boxer, the sweet spot is the bone, heart or paw. All three are Medium, about 1.5 inches, which suits a standard collar and a medium neck without swallowing it. Want it a little bigger? The military runs about 2 inches and gives long sayings room to breathe. Want it smaller? The round is about 1.2 inches. The polished stainless bone is the biggest we make at 48 mm, and it comes with a paw cut-out, which is handy if your Boxer also likes to be noticed. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.

Boxer tag FAQ

Still stuck? hello@saltydogtags.com. A human answers. The dogs supervise.

Which tag shape fits a Boxer?

Tags come in one size per shape, so picking a shape is picking a size. For a Boxer, the bone, heart or paw (all Medium, about 1.5 inches) is the sweet spot. Want it bigger? The military is about 2 inches. Want it smaller? The round is about 1.2 inches.

Will drool and water wear off the engraving?

No. The text is laser engraved into the metal, so there's no ink for drool or water to lift.

Can I put my boxer's name and my phone number on the tag?

Yes. Funny saying on the front, name and phone number on the back.

Do you have sayings written specifically for boxers?

Eight on this page, about the wiggle, the jumping and the never-ending puppy phase. Each fits the tag as written.

Boxers overheat. Should the tag say so?

If your boxer struggles in heat, a back-line note like "Keep me cool" can help a finder. Ask your vet about your own dog's limits.
