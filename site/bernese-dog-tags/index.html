Built for a very large dog with a very soft heart

A Bernese is a 100-pound dog who truly believes he is a lap dog. He will lean on your legs, sit on your feet and lie across the doorway so you cannot leave without stepping over him. If you ignore him for ten minutes, he will sigh like you ended the relationship.

The shedding is its own chapter. A Bernese sheds a second dog every week, and the fur ends up in the soup. Owners just call it seasoning. The drool comes in a close second, and the tag takes all of it, so we laser engrave the text into the metal.

Berners also love cold weather and hate warm days, and they are not marathon runners. A tag that stays readable on a thick tricolor ruff is the job.

Which shape fits your Bernese Mountain Dog

Tags here come in one size per shape, so the shape you pick sets the size. A Bernese Mountain Dog has a big head and a thick collar, and a small tag disappears against all that. Go with the military, about 2 inches, or the polished stainless bone, the biggest we make at 48 mm, with a paw cut-out. Both are easy to read at a glance and easy to find under the fur. The bone, heart and paw are Medium, about 1.5 inches. They work if you like a quieter tag, or if the collar is slim. Leave the round for the lapdogs. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.

Bernese tag FAQ

Still stuck? hello@saltydogtags.com. A human answers. The dogs supervise.

Which tag shape fits a Bernese Mountain Dog?

Tags come in one size per shape, so the shape sets the size. A Bernese Mountain Dog has a big head and a thick collar, so go with the military (about 2 inches) or the polished stainless bone (48 mm, with a paw cut-out). The bone, heart and paw (about 1.5 inches) suit a slimmer collar. Leave the round for the lapdogs.

Will mud, drool and rough play wear off the engraving?

No. The text is laser engraved into the metal, so scuffing doesn't erase it.

Can I put my Bernese's name and my phone number on the tag?

Yes. Saying on the front, name and phone number on the back.

Do you have sayings written specifically for Berners?

12 on this page. Each fits the tag as written.

What should I put on a Bernese Mountain Dog's tag?

One joke for the front, two short lines at most, like 100 lbs. Still a lap dog. Put your dog's name and a phone number on the back. Go with the military or the polished stainless bone so it shows through the mane.

What happens if the engraving comes out wrong?

If we mis-engrave it, we remake it free. Tags are engraved and shipped from Lehi, Utah in 1 to 2 business days, then USPS takes 3 to 5 days in transit. US only.
