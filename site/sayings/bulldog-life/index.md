# Bulldog life, engraved.

> Bulldog dog tag sayings about snoring, drool, loaf shape and gas, written by a family with seven English bulldogs. Engraved from $12, ships from Utah.

Canonical page: https://saltydogtags.com/sayings/bulldog-life/

26 sayings


We have seven English bulldogs, so these aren't guesses. They're field notes. The snoring is real. The drool is real. The loaf shape is real, and so is the look they give you when the walk ends and they decide to lie down in the middle of the sidewalk.

This is the big list: wrinkles, underbite, stubborn streak and the occasional gas event. "Walk ended. I lay down. Negotiate." "Face like a fist. Heart like a pillow." "Built like a loaf." If you own one, you already know which line is yours. If you're shopping for someone who does, any of them will land.

Bulldogs have thick necks and wide collars, so check the size guide before you order: the bone, heart and paw run about 1.5 inches and the military runs about 2 inches. Name and number go on the back. Mis-engrave it and we'll remake it free. Chewed it? The first replacement is $5.
