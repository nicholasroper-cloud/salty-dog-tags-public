# Certified cute dog tag sayings.

> Cute dog tag sayings with a bite: 'Small. Loud. Deeply loved.' 'Pocket-sized problem.' 'Smol. Loud. Yours.' Laser engraved from $12, ships from Utah.

Canonical page: https://saltydogtags.com/sayings/certified-cute/

31 sayings


Cute is a lot of pressure. Your dog has to look at you with the head tilt, carry a tiny bow, and be deeply loved on demand. The least a tag can do is back your dog up.

These sayings are the sweet end of the list, with teeth. "I'm somebody's whole heart." "Soft. Sweet. Slightly unhinged." "Cinder block head. Marshmallow heart." "Head tilt is a manipulation tactic." Cute and a little bit dangerous, which is how we like them. Wearing a bow does not make a dog harmless.

Heart and round tags suit the small ones, and the heart says it before the words do. Pick a line, add the name and your number on the back, and we engrave it in Lehi, Utah. Tags start at $12. If we mis-engrave it, we remake it free. Your dog will accept the credit.
