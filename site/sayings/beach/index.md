Salty is in our name for a reason. A salty dog is a happy dog with sand in places sand shouldn't be. This page is for that dog.

There's the surf dog with zero skills. The one who can't swim but insists on the beach anyway. The sun-dried dog who is spoiled and not sorry. The dog who stole your sandwich and cited beach rules. And one chihuahua who has declared the sand lava and needs to be carried. Respect the lava.

The words are cut into the metal, not printed on top. Our own dogs have worn theirs through salt water, sand and whatever is in the yard. Pick a line, add the name and number on the back, and we'll ship it from Lehi, Utah. Tags start at $12. Sunscreen not included. Salt on the tag is fine. Salt on the couch is your problem.
