Lost and found dog tag sayings.

Dogs wander. Bulldogs wander about forty feet and then lie down. Either way, the back of the tag does the serious work: your dog's name and your phone number, so a stranger has something to dial. The front is where we have fun.

These are our lost-and-found lines. Some are polite. Some are not. "Call my mom. She's freaking out" is for the honest owner. "Not lost. Taking a tour" is for the dog with a different story. "If found, bring snacks" sets expectations early.

Pick a line and load it into the builder. Change a word if you want. We won't be offended. The saying goes on the front, the name and number go on the back, and we send you a photo of the exact tag before it ships. Mis-engraved? We remake it free. Chewed it or lost it? The first replacement is $5 flat.
