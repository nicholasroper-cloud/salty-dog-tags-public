If you can read this, I'm a flight risk.

A warning label for the dog who treats every open door like a prize. If a stranger can read the tag, the dog is already close to leaving. This one earns its place on escape artists, door dashers, and anyone whose dog has learned what a latch sounds like. Keep a leash handy, since the tag is funny and the sprint is real.
