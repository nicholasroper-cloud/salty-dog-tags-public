# Family dog tag sayings.

> Dog tag sayings for the favorite, the baby and the dog with two moms: 'Mom's favorite. Dad's problem.' and more. Engraved from $12, ships from Utah.

Canonical page: https://saltydogtags.com/sayings/family/

25 sayings


Every dog has a rank in the house. Nobody agrees on it. Mom says the baby. Dad says he doesn't have a favorite, loudly, while slipping him cheese. Grandma has already decided.

These are the tags that settle it. "Mom's favorite. Dad's problem." "Has 2 moms. Both worried." "Unpaid nanny. Call my mom." Some are for the dog who runs the house. Some are for the dog who was raised by cats and is still working through it. All of them put the family on the tag, where the whole neighborhood can read it.

Pick one to load it into the builder, then swap "mom" for "dad" or "grandma" if the house runs differently. The name and your number go on the back. Three or more tags in one order get 15% off. We have seven dogs, so we checked the math.
