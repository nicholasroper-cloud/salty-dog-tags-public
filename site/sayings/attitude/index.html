Dog tag sayings with attitude.

Some dogs are sweet. Some dogs are sweet and also a menace. This page is for the second kind. These are the lines for the dog who steals the sandwich, hears cheese from three rooms away and ignores the 200 words you taught him.

Attitude tags work because they're true. Nobody has ever read "Barks first. Thinks never." and doubted it. Nobody has met a "Certified menace" and asked for proof. The tag just says out loud what the neighbors already know, and it saves you a lot of explaining at the park.

Pick one and it loads straight into the builder. Your dog's name and your phone number go on the back, engraved into the metal, not printed on it. Tags start at $12 and ship from Lehi, Utah. If we mis-engrave it, we remake it free. Choose your dog's attitude below, then give it a name.
