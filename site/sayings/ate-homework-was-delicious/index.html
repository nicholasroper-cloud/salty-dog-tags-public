Ate homework. Was delicious.

The oldest excuse in the book, finally true. Your dog did not hand in the assignment. He ate it. Review: excellent. This is a tag for the kid's dog, the teacher's dog, or any dog with a taste for paper, cardboard, and mail. It gets a laugh at the front door and a nod from every parent in the building. Two short lines, easy to read. On the back goes your dog's name and your phone number. Tags start at $12.
