Built like a loaf.

Look at a bulldog from the side. Rectangle. Bread. Somebody sliced the corners off and forgot to bake in a neck. This is the line for the dog who sits in loaf position, paws tucked, and refuses to explain himself. It works on a bulldog, a corgi, a pug or any dog who has ever been mistaken for a cushion. Pairs well with the heart shape, for a loaf with feelings. Your dog's name and number go on the back. Laser engraved, from $12.

Engrave it and let the loaf speak for itself.
