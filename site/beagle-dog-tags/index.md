## Built for a dog that goes wherever the smell goes

A beagle's tag spends its life an inch off the ground: through hedges, under porches, nose-first into every trash bag on the street. Printed tags lose that fight fast.

Our text is laser-cut into the metal. No ink on top, so dirt, brush and the occasional puddle don't take your phone number with them.

## Which shape fits your Beagle

Tags here come in one size per shape, so picking a shape is picking a size. For a Beagle, the sweet spot is the bone, heart or paw. All three are Medium, about 1.5 inches, which suits a standard collar and a medium neck without swallowing it. Want it a little bigger? The military runs about 2 inches and gives long sayings room to breathe. Want it smaller? The round is about 1.2 inches. The polished stainless bone is the biggest we make at 48 mm, and it comes with a paw cut-out, which is handy if your Beagle also likes to be noticed. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.

## Beagle tag FAQ

Still stuck? [hello@saltydogtags.com](mailto:hello@saltydogtags.com). A human answers. The dogs supervise.

**Which tag shape fits a Beagle?**

Tags come in one size per shape, so picking a shape is picking a size. For a Beagle, the bone, heart or paw (all Medium, about 1.5 inches) is the sweet spot. Want it bigger? The military is about 2 inches. Want it smaller? The round is about 1.2 inches.

**Will dirt and digging wear off the engraving?**

No. The words are laser engraved into the metal, not printed on, so scuffs don't erase them.

**Can I put my beagle's name and my phone number on the tag?**

Yes. Saying on the front, name and phone number on the back. For a scent-chaser, a line like "Call mom. Bring cheese." also tells a finder how to catch them.

**Do you have sayings written specifically for beagles?**

Eight on this page, about the nose, the baying and the counter surfing. Each fits the tag as written.

**What happens if the engraving comes out wrong?**

If we mis-engrave it, we remake it free. Tags are engraved and shipped from Lehi, Utah in 1 to 2 business days, then USPS takes 3 to 5 days in transit. US only.
