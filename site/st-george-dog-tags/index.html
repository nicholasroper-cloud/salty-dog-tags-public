St. George Dog Tags, Engraved by a Utah Bulldog Family

We're a bulldog family moving to St. George in early 2027. Until the laser makes the trip, every tag is engraved in Lehi, checked against your order, photographed, and mailed USPS to your St. George or Washington County address. The front gets a joke. The back gets your dog's name and your phone number, cut into the metal by a laser, not printed on top.

Why a St. George dog needs a tag that reads

Down here a loose dog has two problems: the heat and the clock. Utah State Parks says daytime temperatures at Snow Canyon State Park frequently top 105 degrees in July and August. And shelters keep office hours. The St. George Animal Shelter is by appointment only and closed Sundays, and the Washington City shelter is open Monday to Friday, 9 to 4, closed weekends and holidays.

A license number on a Saturday afternoon means waiting for an office to open. Your phone number on the tag means a call right now. That's the whole job of an ID tag: digits a stranger can read and dial. Our desert heat guide has the rest, from the pavement test to hot cars, straight from the park and weather service pages.

Three tags on a Washington County collar

  1. The license tag from your city. In St. George it's $10 for a spayed or neutered dog and $20 otherwise, renewed every year by February 28 (city page). Washington City, Hurricane and Ivins each run their own program, with their own fees. Our St. George license rules guide has all four.
  2. The rabies tag from your vet. Hurricane spells it out: a license and rabies tag must be attached to your dog's collar (Hurricane City).
  3. An ID tag with your number. That's the one we make. It's an ID tag, not a license or rabies tag, so it doesn't replace either of the other two.

Want a backup? On tags that support it, a $5 scan-to-find QR goes on the back. It opens a finder page for your dog, with email alerts to you once you activate it. The phone number stays engraved too, because a QR needs a signal.

Shipping to St. George

We engrave and ship within 1 to 2 business days of payment, and USPS takes about 3 to 5 days in transit from Lehi to Washington County. Shipping is $4.50 and free on orders of $35 or more. Utah orders include Utah sales tax. Need it by a date, like a gotcha day or the first Snow Canyon hike? Put the deadline in the order notes and we'll tell you straight whether we can make it.

Where St. George dogs run

The City of St. George lists three fenced dog parks, and the neighbors have their own. Our dog parks and trails guide has the addresses and the rules from each city, state and park page:

  • St. George: Firehouse, J.C. Snow and Riverbend parks
  • Washington City: Dog Town Dog Park
  • Hurricane: the city dog park
  • Snow Canyon State Park: every trail, on a 6-foot leash
  • Red Cliffs Desert Reserve: leash on, stay on the trail
  • Zion: the Pa'rus Trail only

Bring a leash even to the dog park. Every set of rules on that list wants it on at the gate.

Around Washington County

Rules change at the city line, so we keep each city in its own section of the guides instead of pretending one set fits all:

  • Washington City licenses dogs for two years ($20 altered, $40 unaltered on the city's fee schedule) and wants dogs on a 6-foot leash in city parks. See Washington City license rules and Dog Town Dog Park.
  • Hurricane wants a license by 4 months and the license and rabies tag on the collar. See Hurricane license rules.
  • Ivins and Santa Clara share one public safety department, with an animal control office in Ivins. An Ivins license lasts until the rabies certificate expires. See Ivins and Santa Clara.

Elsewhere in Utah? The Utah dog tags page covers the Wasatch Front.

Questions humans ask

St. George questions

Still stuck? hello@saltydogtags.com. A human answers. The dogs supervise.

Where are your tags engraved?

In Lehi, Utah, for now. We're moving to St. George in early 2027, and until then every tag is engraved in Lehi and mailed USPS. There is no walk-in shop.

How fast does a tag get to St. George?

We engrave and ship within 1 to 2 business days of payment, then USPS takes 3 to 5 days in transit. Shipping is $4.50 and free on orders of $35 or more. Utah sales tax is added to Utah orders.

Is a Salty Dog Tag a St. George dog license?

No. It's an ID tag with your phone number, not a license or rabies tag. The City of St. George issues the license tag; our guide to St. George and Washington County license rules has the fees and deadlines from the city pages.

Do you have a QR tag?

Yes. For $5 more, a scan-to-find QR goes on the back of tags that support it. Scanning it opens a finder page for your dog, and once you activate it you get an email alert when someone scans it or sends a message. Your phone number stays engraved too, because a QR needs a signal.

Can I write my own St. George saying?

Yes. You get up to three lines on the front, 24 characters each, and the preview shows exactly what fits. The name and number go on the back. If a line runs long, we'll email before we engrave.
