## Why a St. George dog needs a tag that reads

Down here a loose dog has two problems: the heat and the clock. Utah State Parks says daytime temperatures at [Snow Canyon State Park](https://stateparks.utah.gov/parks/snow-canyon/current-conditions/) frequently top 105 degrees in July and August. And shelters keep office hours. The [St. George Animal Shelter](https://www.sgcityutah.gov/departments/police_department/animal_services___shelter.php) is by appointment only and closed Sundays, and the [Washington City shelter](https://washingtonutah.gov/services/publicsafety/police/animalwelfare) is open Monday to Friday, 9 to 4, closed weekends and holidays.

A license number on a Saturday afternoon means waiting for an office to open. Your phone number on the tag means a call right now. That's the whole job of an ID tag: digits a stranger can read and dial. Our [desert heat guide](https://saltydogtags.com/guides/desert-heat-dog-safety-st-george/) has the rest, from the pavement test to hot cars, straight from the park and weather service pages.

## Three tags on a Washington County collar

- **The license tag** from your city. In St. George it's $10 for a spayed or neutered dog and $20 otherwise, renewed every year by February 28 ([city page](https://sgcityutah.gov/doglicense/)). Washington City, Hurricane and Ivins each run their own program, with their own fees. Our [St. George license rules guide](https://saltydogtags.com/guides/st-george-dog-license-rules/) has all four.
- **The rabies tag** from your vet. Hurricane spells it out: a license and rabies tag must be attached to your dog's collar ([Hurricane City](https://www.cityofhurricane.com/159/Animal-Control-Shelter)).
- **An ID tag with your number.** That's the one we make. It's an ID tag, not a license or rabies tag, so it doesn't replace either of the other two.

Want a backup? On tags that support it, a $5 scan-to-find QR goes on the back. It opens a finder page for your dog, with email alerts to you once you activate it. The phone number stays engraved too, because a QR needs a signal.

## Around Washington County

Rules change at the city line, so we keep each city in its own section of the guides instead of pretending one set fits all:

- **Washington City** licenses dogs for two years ($20 altered, $40 unaltered on the city's fee schedule) and wants dogs on a 6-foot leash in city parks. See [Washington City license rules](https://saltydogtags.com/guides/st-george-dog-license-rules/#washington-city) and [Dog Town Dog Park](https://saltydogtags.com/guides/st-george-dog-parks-and-trails/#washington-city).
- **Hurricane** wants a license by 4 months and the license and rabies tag on the collar. See [Hurricane license rules](https://saltydogtags.com/guides/st-george-dog-license-rules/#hurricane).
- **Ivins and Santa Clara** share one public safety department, with an animal control office in Ivins. An Ivins license lasts until the rabies certificate expires. See [Ivins and Santa Clara](https://saltydogtags.com/guides/st-george-dog-license-rules/#ivins-and-santa-clara).

Elsewhere in Utah? The [Utah dog tags page](https://saltydogtags.com/utah-dog-tags/) covers the Wasatch Front.
