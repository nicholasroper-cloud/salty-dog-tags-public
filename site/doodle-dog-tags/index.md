## Pond water, humidity and the groomer can't take it

A doodle doesn't get wet. It absorbs. Pool water, pond water, sprinkler water, the humidity on a "dry" afternoon. All of it soaks into the fur, and the tag sits right in the middle of it for hours.

That's why we engrave instead of print. The words are cut into the tag with a laser, so there's no ink to wash out, fade or peel.

Your dog will still smell like a wet couch cushion. The tag will still be readable.

## Which shape fits your Doodle

Tags here come in one size per shape, so the shape you pick sets the size. A Doodle has a big head and a thick collar, and a small tag disappears against all that. Go with the military, about 2 inches, or the polished stainless bone, the biggest we make at 48 mm, with a paw cut-out. Both are easy to read at a glance and easy to find under the fur. The bone, heart and paw are Medium, about 1.5 inches. They work if you like a quieter tag, or if the collar is slim. Leave the round for the lapdogs. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.

## Goldendoodle & Labradoodle tag FAQ

Still stuck? [hello@saltydogtags.com](mailto:hello@saltydogtags.com). A human answers. The dogs supervise.

**Which tag shape fits a Doodle?**

Tags come in one size per shape, so the shape sets the size. A Doodle has a big head and a thick collar, so go with the military (about 2 inches) or the polished stainless bone (48 mm, with a paw cut-out). The bone, heart and paw (about 1.5 inches) suit a slimmer collar. Leave the round for the lapdogs.

**Will a tag get lost in my doodle's fur?**

A small one can. Doodle coats grow right over little tags, so go with the military or the polished stainless bone, which are big enough to stay visible.

**Will pool water, salt water or humidity ruin an engraved tag?**

No. The words are laser engraved into the metal, not printed on top, so there's no ink to wash off.

**Can I put my doodle's name and my phone number on the tag?**

Yes, that's the standard build. The funny saying goes on the front, and your dog's name and your phone number are engraved on the back.

**What happens if the engraving comes out wrong?**
