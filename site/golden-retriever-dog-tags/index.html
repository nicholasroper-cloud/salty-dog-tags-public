Pool water, pond water, every water

A golden doesn't go swimming so much as refuse to leave. Pool, lake, canal, the sprinkler, the dog bowl with one paw in it. Add humid summers and a tag spends most of its life damp.

That's why we laser engrave instead of printing. The words are cut into the metal, so there's no ink to fade, chip or peel off after the four hundredth dip.

Your dog will come out of the water smelling like a swamp. The tag will still be readable.

Which shape fits your Golden Retriever

Tags here come in one size per shape, so the shape you pick sets the size. A Golden Retriever has a big head and a thick collar, and a small tag disappears against all that. Go with the military, about 2 inches, or the polished stainless bone, the biggest we make at 48 mm, with a paw cut-out. Both are easy to read at a glance and easy to find under the fur. The bone, heart and paw are Medium, about 1.5 inches. They work if you like a quieter tag, or if the collar is slim. Leave the round for the lapdogs. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.

Golden Retriever tag FAQ

Still stuck? hello@saltydogtags.com. A human answers. The dogs supervise.

Which tag shape fits a Golden Retriever?

Tags come in one size per shape, so the shape sets the size. A Golden Retriever has a big head and a thick collar, so go with the military (about 2 inches) or the polished stainless bone (48 mm, with a paw cut-out). The bone, heart and paw (about 1.5 inches) suit a slimmer collar. Leave the round for the lapdogs.

Will pool, salt or pond water ruin an engraved golden retriever tag?

No. The words are laser engraved into the metal, not printed on top, so there's nothing for chlorine, salt water or the retention pond to wash off.

Can I put my golden retriever's name and my phone number on the tag?

Yes, that's the standard build. The funny saying goes on the front, and your dog's name plus your phone number are engraved on the back. A third back line is optional.

Do you have sayings written specifically for golden retrievers?

Nine of them on this page, about the shedding, the swimming, the shoe theft and the total lack of guard-dog instinct. Every one fits the tag as written, and the full sayings list has hundreds more.

What happens if the engraving comes out wrong?

Custom tags aren't returnable, but if we mis-engrave it, we remake it free. Send a photo and we re-cut it. Tags are engraved and shipped from Lehi, Utah in 1 to 2 business days, then USPS takes 3 to 5 days in transit. US only.
