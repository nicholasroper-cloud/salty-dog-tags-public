## Built for the softest dog in the room

A Cavalier has one setting, and it is love. They have no prey drive, no guard instinct and no shame about climbing onto the stranger who just came to fix the dishwasher. If you sit down, a Cavalier is already on you, and it will stay there until you need to stand.

## Which shape fits your Cavalier

Tags here come in one size per shape, so picking a shape is picking a size. For a Cavalier, the sweet spot is the bone, heart or paw. All three are Medium, about 1.5 inches, which suits a standard collar and a medium neck without swallowing it. Want it a little bigger? The military runs about 2 inches and gives long sayings room to breathe. Want it smaller? The round is about 1.2 inches. The polished stainless bone is the biggest we make at 48 mm, and it comes with a paw cut-out, which is handy if your Cavalier also likes to be noticed. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.
