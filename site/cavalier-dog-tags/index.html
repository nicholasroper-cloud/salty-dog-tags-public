Built for the softest dog in the room

A Cavalier has one setting, and it is love. They have no prey drive, no guard instinct and no shame about climbing onto the stranger who just came to fix the dishwasher. If you sit down, a Cavalier is already on you, and it will stay there until you need to stand.

Those pleading eyes work on everyone, and you will lose every staring contest at dinner. They are also people-pleasers who follow you from room to room, so the tag spends its day jingling right behind you.

A Cavalier's silky ears also drag through the water bowl, so a tag that holds up to drool and dinner is a real need. Ours is laser engraved into the metal, so it stays readable.

Which shape fits your Cavalier

Tags here come in one size per shape, so picking a shape is picking a size. For a Cavalier, the sweet spot is the bone, heart or paw. All three are Medium, about 1.5 inches, which suits a standard collar and a medium neck without swallowing it. Want it a little bigger? The military runs about 2 inches and gives long sayings room to breathe. Want it smaller? The round is about 1.2 inches. The polished stainless bone is the biggest we make at 48 mm, and it comes with a paw cut-out, which is handy if your Cavalier also likes to be noticed. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.

Still stuck? hello@saltydogtags.com. A human answers. The dogs supervise.

Which tag shape fits a Cavalier?

Tags come in one size per shape, so picking a shape is picking a size. For a Cavalier, the bone, heart or paw (all Medium, about 1.5 inches) is the sweet spot. Want it bigger? The military is about 2 inches. Want it smaller? The round is about 1.2 inches.

Will mud, drool and rough play wear off the engraving?

No. The text is laser engraved into the metal, so scuffing doesn't erase it.

Can I put my Cavalier's name and my phone number on the tag?

Yes. Saying on the front, name and phone number on the back.

Do you have sayings written specifically for Cavaliers?

12 on this page. Each fits the tag as written.

What should I put on a Cavalier's tag?

One joke for the front, two short lines at most, like Lap barnacle. Remove with snacks. Put your dog's name and a phone number on the back. A Cavalier will go home with anyone, so the number is the whole point.

What happens if the engraving comes out wrong?

If we mis-engrave it, we remake it free. Tags are engraved and shipped from Lehi, Utah in 1 to 2 business days, then USPS takes 3 to 5 days in transit. US only.
