## Terms of sale

- **Who you're buying from:** Salty Dog Tags, a one-person shop in Lehi, Utah.
- **Prices** are in US dollars, as shown at checkout. The 15% off 3+ tags applies to the base tag price, not color or photo add-ons. Salty Maze upgrade codes are one use, good 30 days, and need a stainless, polished or brass tag.
- **Sales tax:** Utah sales tax is added to orders shipped to a Utah address. We don't collect tax for other states right now.
- **Paying:** nothing is charged when you send an order. We email you a secure Clover invoice, usually within a day, and you pay it with any card or Apple Pay. We never see your card number. By sending an order you agree to these policies.
- **Your order is accepted** once you've paid and we've approved any uploaded art. If we decline it (see Artwork), you pay nothing or get a full refund. You must be 18 or older to order, or have a parent or guardian order for you.
- **Proofs:** photo tags get an emailed proof before engraving. Text that won't fit gets an email first. Every tag is photographed before it ships.
- **Custom designs ("We design it" on full-color tags):** Bold type is free. The mascot, synthwave and photo styles add a $2 design fee per design. We start the design after your invoice is paid, email you a proof, and include one round of changes. If you still don't like it, we print your saying in Bold type at no extra charge instead. The design fee isn't refunded once we've sent the proof.
- **Bulldog Promise:** see Returns & remakes.
- **What we owe:** at most, what you paid for that order. These are ID tags, not GPS, so keep the info current. Nothing here takes away rights the law gives you.

## Shipping

- **US addresses only:** the 50 states and DC. No APO/FPO or territories right now.
- **$4.50 flat USPS**, free when your order is $35+ after discounts, before tax.
- **Timing:** we engrave and ship from Lehi within 1 to 2 business days of payment (photo tags and custom designs: of proof approval), then USPS takes about 3 to 5 days in transit. If we'll miss that window, we'll email you, and you can cancel for a full refund.
- **Lost or damaged in transit:** email your order number within 30 days of the expected delivery date. We'll deal with USPS and remake it free. No waiting on the claim.
- **Wrong address:** if the address you entered was wrong, a reship or remake is priced like a typo remake.

## Returns & remakes

Every tag and ornament is made just for you, so no returns. Instead:

- **Our mistake:** if the engraving doesn't match your order, or the tag is defective, email a photo within 30 days of delivery. Free remake, free shipping.
- **Your typo:** we engrave exactly what you ordered, so check spelling and phone number twice. If the typo was yours, we remake at a discount.
- **Chewed or lost:** first replacement $5 flat.
- **Full-color printed tags.** Printed color isn't as tough as engraving. On a working collar it scuffs and fades with wear, and faster with chewing, swimming and rough play. Normal wear isn't a defect. If the print peels or flakes within 30 days of delivery, that's on us: free remake.
- **Gifts.** The buyer is our customer for the invoice, approvals and remakes. A gift recipient can email us the order number to get a remake under the same rules. We use a recipient's name and address only to deliver.
- **Refunds** go back to your original card through Clover. Your bank sets the timing, usually a few business days.

## Cancellations

Cancel anytime before your tag is engraved: email your order number and you'll get a full refund. Once the laser runs, the tag is yours, unless we miss the shipping window above.

## User content & 18+

- **What you send:** you're responsible for your text and photos. Only upload photos you took or may use, and skip logos you don't have rights to. You let us use them to make your tag and run the roast. We can refuse anything hateful, threatening, harassing or infringing, and refund you.
- **Roast results are written by AI.** They can be wrong, weird, or over the line. Read every word before ordering. We engrave what you order.
- **Salty 18+** has real swear words and is for adults only. You confirm you're 18+ before it opens, and your browser remembers that. Ordering an 18+ saying means you're 18 or older. "Ruder than I expected" isn't a remake reason.
- **Tag us:** when you tag us in a post of your dog or email us a photo, you let us repost it with credit on our site (including the Wall of Shame) and our socials, free. You still own it. Ask and we'll take it down from our site and accounts within 7 days. We can't recall copies others shared. Please don't send photos of other people's kids.

## Your artwork & photos

- **What you can send:** photos you took, art you made, or images whose owner gave you permission to have them printed. Not allowed: logos, team or brand names, cartoon or movie characters, celebrity or public-figure photos, or someone else's photo or art (including another person's pet pic from social media) without their permission.
- **People in photos:** fine, if everyone shown agreed to be on it. For anyone under 18, a parent or guardian must agree.
- **You keep ownership.** You let us store, crop, resize, color-correct and print your image to make, ship and remake your order. Nothing else: no ads and no social posts unless you separately tell us yes in writing. (Tag us, above, is separate.)
- **Your promise:** ticking the artwork box confirms the rules above. If that turns out to be untrue and someone brings a claim against us over your image or text, you agree to cover our reasonable costs of that claim, including any amount we have to pay.
- **What we decline:** we check every upload by hand. We decline art we believe infringes someone's rights; nudity or sexual content; any 18+ wording or saying next to a person who looks under 18; and anything hateful, threatening or harassing. Swear words in your own art follow the Salty 18+ rule: adults only. Declining isn't an accusation, and we don't have to give legal reasons.
- **If we decline:** we email you within 2 business days. Not paid yet: the invoice is cancelled, nothing is charged, and you can send new art or pick a house style. Already paid: we refund the declined item, plus its share of shipping and tax, to your card within 3 business days. Or cancel the whole order for a full refund. We never print declined art.
- **Rights complaints:** if you think something we made uses your work or your brand, email hello@saltydogtags.com with what it is and how to reach you. We stop making anything we agree infringes, and we reply within 10 business days.
- **How long we keep images:** uploaded images are deleted within 120 days of your order, and declined images within 7 days. We keep a record that you ticked the box, when you ticked it, and a fingerprint of the file for 3 years.

## Monthly photo contest

**Short version:** each month we pick our favorite dog face and give that dog a free tag. No purchase necessary.

- **No purchase necessary.** You don't have to buy anything to enter or to win. Buying a tag does not improve your chances.
- **How to enter:** post a photo of your dog on Instagram, TikTok or Facebook with the hashtag **#SaltyDogTags**, or email the photo to [hello@saltydogtags.com](mailto:hello@saltydogtags.com?subject=Tag%20pic). The dog can be wearing a Salty Dog Tag or any other tag.
- **Who can enter:** residents of the United States who are 18 or older, or a parent or guardian entering for a child.
- **How the winner is chosen:** this is a judged pick, not a random drawing. One winner each month, chosen by the owners of Salty Dog Tags for the best face. Entries are judged on the dog's face and nothing else, so the number of entries does not decide who wins.
- **The prize:** one free tag, in a shape of our choice. No cash value, no substitutions and no transfers.
- **Reposting:** entering lets us repost your photo with credit, as described under Tag us above.
- **Void where prohibited.**
- **Sponsor:** Salty Dog Tags, Lehi, Utah.

## Governing law

Utah law governs these policies and every sale, without regard to conflict-of-law rules. Email us first; most things get fixed in one reply. If not, disputes go to the state or federal courts in Utah County, Utah, or to small claims court. None of this removes consumer protections you have where you live.

The version of these policies posted when you order applies to that order.
