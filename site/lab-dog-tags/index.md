## Built for a dog that lives in the water

A lab doesn't go for a swim. A lab goes in. Pool, lake, canal, the bay, the cooler you left open. Then it rolls in the sand to dry off and goes back in. A printed tag doesn't last a summer like that.

Ours are laser engraved, so the words are cut into the metal. There's no ink for chlorine, salt water or mud to wash off, because the lettering isn't sitting on top.

Your lab will still smell like a pond. The tag will still be readable.

## Which shape fits your Labrador

Tags here come in one size per shape, so the shape you pick sets the size. A Labrador has a big head and a thick collar, and a small tag disappears against all that. Go with the military, about 2 inches, or the polished stainless bone, the biggest we make at 48 mm, with a paw cut-out. Both are easy to read at a glance and easy to find under the fur. The bone, heart and paw are Medium, about 1.5 inches. They work if you like a quieter tag, or if the collar is slim. Leave the round for the lapdogs. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.

## Lab tag FAQ

Still stuck? [hello@saltydogtags.com](mailto:hello@saltydogtags.com). A human answers. The dogs supervise.

**Which tag shape fits a Labrador?**

Tags come in one size per shape, so the shape sets the size. A Labrador has a big head and a thick collar, so go with the military (about 2 inches) or the polished stainless bone (48 mm, with a paw cut-out). The bone, heart and paw (about 1.5 inches) suit a slimmer collar. Leave the round for the lapdogs.

**Will pool water and salt water ruin an engraved lab tag?**

No. The words are laser engraved into the metal, not printed on it, so there's no ink for chlorine, salt or pond water to wash away. Swim season can run all year.

**Can I put my lab's name and my phone number on the tag?**

Yes, that's the standard build. The funny saying goes on the front, and your dog's name plus your phone number are engraved on the back. A third back line is optional.

**Do you have sayings written specifically for Labradors?**

Nine of them on this page, about the eating, the swimming, the shedding, the fetching and the tail that clears coffee tables. Every one fits the tag as written, and the full sayings list has hundreds more.

**What happens if the engraving comes out wrong?**

Custom tags aren't returnable, but if we mis-engrave it, we remake it free. Send a photo and we re-cut it. Tags are engraved and shipped from Lehi, Utah in 1 to 2 business days, then USPS takes 3 to 5 days in transit. US only.
