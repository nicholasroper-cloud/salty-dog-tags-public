## Built for the dog that already has a getaway plan

If any breed is going to test the back of its tag, it's a husky. They dig under fences, slip collars, and treat a closed gate as a puzzle with a time limit. The tag is the only thing standing between a stranger and a very loud mystery.

That's why the text has to stay readable. Ours is laser-cut into the metal, with no paint on top to chip, so the phone number survives digging, snow, mud and whatever creek they found on the way out of the neighborhood.

If found, the tag does the talking. The husky will also do the talking.

## Which shape fits your Husky

Tags here come in one size per shape, so the shape you pick sets the size. A Husky has a big head and a thick collar, and a small tag disappears against all that. Go with the military, about 2 inches, or the polished stainless bone, the biggest we make at 48 mm, with a paw cut-out. Both are easy to read at a glance and easy to find under the fur. The bone, heart and paw are Medium, about 1.5 inches. They work if you like a quieter tag, or if the collar is slim. Leave the round for the lapdogs. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.

## Husky tag FAQ

Still stuck? [hello@saltydogtags.com](mailto:hello@saltydogtags.com). A human answers. The dogs supervise.

**Which tag shape fits a Husky?**

Tags come in one size per shape, so the shape sets the size. A Husky has a big head and a thick collar, so go with the military (about 2 inches) or the polished stainless bone (48 mm, with a paw cut-out). The bone, heart and paw (about 1.5 inches) suit a slimmer collar. Leave the round for the lapdogs.

**Will snow, mud and digging wear off the engraving?**

No. The text is laser engraved into the metal, not printed on top, so there's nothing to scrape or wash off.

**Can I put my husky's name and my phone number on the tag?**

Yes, that's the standard build: saying on the front, your dog's name and your phone number on the back. For an escape artist, put the number first and make it big.

**Do you have sayings written specifically for huskies?**

Eight on this page, about the escapes, the screaming and the shedding. Each fits the tag as written. The full sayings list has hundreds more.

**What happens if the engraving comes out wrong?**

Custom tags aren't returnable, but if we mis-engrave it, we remake it free. Tags are engraved and shipped from Lehi, Utah in 1 to 2 business days, then USPS takes 3 to 5 days in transit. US only.
