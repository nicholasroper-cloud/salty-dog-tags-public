## Which shape fits your Doberman

Tags here come in one size per shape, so the shape you pick sets the size. A Doberman has a big head and a thick collar, and a small tag disappears against all that. Go with the military, about 2 inches, or the polished stainless bone, the biggest we make at 48 mm, with a paw cut-out. Both are easy to read at a glance and easy to find under the fur. The bone, heart and paw are Medium, about 1.5 inches. They work if you like a quieter tag, or if the collar is slim. Leave the round for the lapdogs. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.
