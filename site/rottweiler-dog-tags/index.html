Which shape fits your Rottweiler

Tags here come in one size per shape, so the shape you pick sets the size. A Rottweiler has a big head and a thick collar, and a small tag disappears against all that. Go with the military, about 2 inches, or the polished stainless bone, the biggest we make at 48 mm, with a paw cut-out. Both are easy to read at a glance and easy to find under the fur. The bone, heart and paw are Medium, about 1.5 inches. They work if you like a quieter tag, or if the collar is slim. Leave the round for the lapdogs. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.

Rottweiler tag FAQ

Still stuck? hello@saltydogtags.com. A human answers. The dogs supervise.

Which tag shape fits a Rottweiler?

Tags come in one size per shape, so the shape sets the size. A Rottweiler has a big head and a thick collar, so go with the military (about 2 inches) or the polished stainless bone (48 mm, with a paw cut-out). The bone, heart and paw (about 1.5 inches) suit a slimmer collar. Leave the round for the lapdogs.

Will drool, pool water or humidity ruin an engraved tag?

No. The words are cut into the metal with a laser, not printed on top, so there's no ink to wash off or peel away.

Can I put my rottweiler's name and my phone number on the tag?

Yes, that's the standard build. The funny saying goes on the front, and your dog's name and your phone number are engraved on the back.

Do you have sayings written specifically for rottweilers?

Eight on this page, about the guard-dog face, the lap-dog energy, the lean and the wiggle. All clean enough for the vet's office.

What happens if the engraving comes out wrong?

If we mis-engrave it, we remake it free. Tags are engraved and shipped from Lehi, Utah in 1 to 2 business days, then USPS takes 3 to 5 days in transit. US only.
