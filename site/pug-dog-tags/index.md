## Which shape fits your Pug

Tags here come in one size per shape, so picking a shape is picking a size. For a Pug, the sweet spot is the bone, heart or paw. All three are Medium, about 1.5 inches, which suits a standard collar and a medium neck without swallowing it. Want it a little bigger? The military runs about 2 inches and gives long sayings room to breathe. Want it smaller? The round is about 1.2 inches. The polished stainless bone is the biggest we make at 48 mm, and it comes with a paw cut-out, which is handy if your Pug also likes to be noticed. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.

## Pug tag FAQ

Still stuck? [hello@saltydogtags.com](mailto:hello@saltydogtags.com). A human answers. The dogs supervise.

**Which tag shape fits a Pug?**

Tags come in one size per shape, so picking a shape is picking a size. For a Pug, the bone, heart or paw (all Medium, about 1.5 inches) is the sweet spot. Want it bigger? The military is about 2 inches. Want it smaller? The round is about 1.2 inches.

**Will humidity, pool water or drool ruin an engraved pug tag?**

No. The words are laser engraved into the metal, not printed on top, so there's no ink to wash off.

**Can I put my pug's name and my phone number on the tag?**

Yes, that's the standard build. The funny saying goes on the front, and your dog's name and your phone number are engraved on the back.
