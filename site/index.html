Questions humans ask

Straight answers.

Still stuck? hello@saltydogtags.com. A human answers. The dogs supervise.

How do I pay?

After you send the order we email you a Clover invoice. It's a secure payment page from the same company that runs our card readers. Any card works. We never see your card number.

How long does it take?

We engrave and ship within 1 to 2 business days of payment, then USPS takes 3 to 5 days in transit. US only. If it will take longer, we email you first. If you have a deadline, say so in the notes and we'll tell you straight.

What if it's wrong?

Engraved means it's custom to you, so no returns on custom tags. If we made a mistake, or the engraving isn't right, send a photo and we remake it free.

What is the Bulldog Promise?

Three things, in writing. If we mis-engrave your tag, we remake it free. If your dog chews it or loses it, the first replacement is $5 flat. And before your tag ships, we send you a photo of the exact tag, so there are no surprises. That's the whole promise. No fine print hiding behind it. We have seven bulldogs, so we know what happens to tags.

Can I write my own saying?

Yes, and please do. Up to three lines on the front, 24 characters each. The name and number go on the back. The preview shows exactly what fits. If it's too long we'll email you before we engrave.

Will the engraving wear off?

No. It's cut into the metal, not printed on top. The colored tags are anodized aluminum, so the laser removes the color and exposes bright silver underneath. Stainless and brass get a deep dark mark. Our own dogs have worn these through salt water, sand and whatever is in the yard.

Can you put my dog's photo on it?

Yes, on polished stainless. Upload a clear, well-lit photo and we engrave it in grayscale on the front; the name and number still go on the back. We email you a proof before we touch the laser, because a bad photo makes a bad tag and we'd rather tell you first.

What's the color stuff?

Our laser can mark color directly into stainless steel, no ink or paint. The color is grown into the steel by the laser; it's not a coating, so it wears like the engraving does. It's new, so we recommend it on the front and off anything that takes a beating.

Do you make cat tags?

Yes. The 32 mm round or the heart sit fine on a cat collar. The cat will still not wear it, but that's between you and the cat.

Where do the bulldog puppies come in?

Separate thing, same family. We breed English bulldogs occasionally. Email us if you're interested and we'll talk. Tags ship to anyone; puppies don't.
