Built for a dog that never clocks out

A German shepherd doesn't wear a tag. It takes the tag on patrol: fence-line sprints, pool laps in July, wrestling matches with the other dog, and a lot of scratching at a collar buried under a coat that never actually stops shedding.

Ours are laser engraved, so the words are cut into the metal instead of painted on top. On colored tags the laser lifts the anodized color and exposes bright silver underneath; on stainless and brass the mark goes dark. Mud, pool water and a full shedding season can't peel off something that isn't stuck on.

The joke outlasts the undercoat. Which is saying something.

Which shape fits your German Shepherd

Tags here come in one size per shape, so the shape you pick sets the size. A German Shepherd has a big head and a thick collar, and a small tag disappears against all that. Go with the military, about 2 inches, or the polished stainless bone, the biggest we make at 48 mm, with a paw cut-out. Both are easy to read at a glance and easy to find under the fur. The bone, heart and paw are Medium, about 1.5 inches. They work if you like a quieter tag, or if the collar is slim. Leave the round for the lapdogs. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.

German Shepherd tag FAQ

Still stuck? hello@saltydogtags.com. A human answers. The dogs supervise.

Which tag shape fits a German Shepherd?

Tags come in one size per shape, so the shape sets the size. A German Shepherd has a big head and a thick collar, so go with the military (about 2 inches) or the polished stainless bone (48 mm, with a paw cut-out). The bone, heart and paw (about 1.5 inches) suit a slimmer collar. Leave the round for the lapdogs.

Will shedding, swimming and mud wear off the engraving?

No. The words are laser engraved into the metal, not printed on top, so there's no ink to rub, wash or sweat off. Fur, pool water and mud don't erase an engraving.

Can I put my shepherd's name and my phone number on the tag?

Yes. The funny saying goes on the front, and your dog's name plus your phone number are engraved on the back. A third back line is optional.

Do you have sayings written specifically for German shepherds?

Eight on this page, about the ears, the shedding, the herding and the running commentary. Every one fits the tag as written, and the full sayings list has hundreds more.

What happens if the engraving comes out wrong?

Custom tags aren't returnable, but if we mis-engrave it, we remake it free. Send a photo and we re-cut it. Tags are engraved and shipped from Lehi, Utah in 1 to 2 business days, then USPS takes 3 to 5 days in transit. US only.
