We're a family in Lehi, Utah with seven English bulldogs, one laser engraver, and strong opinions about what a dog tag should say. The front gets a joke. The back gets your dog's name and your phone number. Every tag is engraved here in Utah County, checked against your order, photographed, and mailed out USPS. No warehouse, no middleman. Just our laser and the post office.

## Engraved in Lehi

Lehi is where the laser lives. It's a valley town with a mountain on one side and a lake on the other, and a lot of new yards with dogs working out where the gate is. Our seven bulldogs supervise. They don't help, but they inspect every finished tag, mostly by trying to eat it. If you live in Lehi, American Fork, Saratoga Springs, Orem, Provo or Draper, you're practically next door, and your tag has a short trip to your mailbox.

## Built for Utah dogs

Utah weather asks a lot of a dog. Snow in April, a scorching July, a trailhead colder than the parking lot. The tag stays on the collar through all of it, doing one quiet job: giving a stranger your number. Our own dogs have worn theirs through salt water, sand and whatever is in the yard, and the words are cut into the metal, not printed on top.

## Licenses are a separate tag

Your dog's license tag comes from your city or county. The ID tag with your number comes from us. It's an ID tag, not a license or rabies tag. We wrote down the official rules for [Utah dog licenses in Lehi and Salt Lake County](https://saltydogtags.com/guides/utah-dog-license-and-tag-rules/) so you don't have to hunt for them.
