Custom Funny Dog Tags From a Lehi Bulldog Family

We're a family in Lehi, Utah with seven English bulldogs, one laser engraver, and strong opinions about what a dog tag should say. The front gets a joke. The back gets your dog's name and your phone number. Every tag is engraved here in Utah County, checked against your order, photographed, and mailed out USPS. No warehouse, no middleman. Just our laser and the post office.

Engraved in Lehi

Lehi is where the laser lives. It's a valley town with a mountain on one side and a lake on the other, and a lot of new yards with dogs working out where the gate is. Our seven bulldogs supervise. They don't help, but they inspect every finished tag, mostly by trying to eat it. If you live in Lehi, American Fork, Saratoga Springs, Orem, Provo or Draper, you're practically next door, and your tag has a short trip to your mailbox.

Built for Utah dogs

Utah weather asks a lot of a dog. Snow in April, a scorching July, a trailhead colder than the parking lot. The tag stays on the collar through all of it, doing one quiet job: giving a stranger your number. Our own dogs have worn theirs through salt water, sand and whatever is in the yard, and the words are cut into the metal, not printed on top.

Fast across the Wasatch Front

We engrave and ship from Lehi within 1 to 2 business days of payment, and USPS takes about 3 to 5 days in transit. A Wasatch Front address, anywhere from Provo up through Salt Lake to Ogden, is a short trip from our laser. Shipping is $4.50 and free over $35. Utah orders include Utah sales tax. Need it by a date, like a birthday, a trip to the mountains or a gotcha day? Put the deadline in the order notes and we'll tell you straight whether we can make it.

Where to run the tag-wearing dog

Your dog's tag should be on before the first off-leash sprint. Our Utah County dog parks guide checks each park against its city page, so you can skip the guessing:

  • Lehi: Willow Park and Exchange Park
  • Saratoga Springs: Saratoga Ridge Park
  • Orem: Timpanogos Park
  • Provo: Bicentennial Park
  • Draper: Dayland, Galena and West Bluff off-leash areas
  • American Fork and everywhere else
  • Before you go: leash, water, bags

Bring a leash even to the dog park. You'll need it in the parking lot. Headed south? We did the same for St. George dog parks and trails.

Licenses are a separate tag

Your dog's license tag comes from your city or county. The ID tag with your number comes from us. It's an ID tag, not a license or rabies tag. We wrote down the official rules for Utah dog licenses in Lehi and Salt Lake County so you don't have to hunt for them. Down in Washington County, see dog tags for St. George and the St. George license rules.

Questions humans ask

Utah questions

Still stuck? hello@saltydogtags.com. A human answers. The dogs supervise.

Where are Salty Dog Tags engraved?

In Lehi, Utah, by a family with seven English bulldogs and one laser engraver. Every tag is engraved here, photographed for you before it ships, and mailed USPS.

How fast will my tag get to me in Utah?

We engrave and ship within 1 to 2 business days of payment, then USPS takes 3 to 5 days in transit. Wasatch Front addresses are a short trip from Lehi. Every tag is photographed before it goes out, so you'll know it's on the way.

Do you ship outside Utah?

Yes, anywhere in the US. Shipping is $4.50 and free on orders over $35. Utah sales tax is added to Utah orders.

Is a Salty Dog Tag a Utah dog license?

No. It's an ID tag with your phone number, not a license or rabies tag. Your city or county issues those. Our guide to Utah dog license and tag rules walks through Lehi and Salt Lake County.

Can I write my own Utah saying?

Yes, and please do. You get up to three lines on the front, 24 characters each, and the preview shows exactly what fits. The name and number go on the back. If a line runs long, we'll email before we engrave.
