## Built for drool, humidity and the occasional water-bowl incident

A frenchie doesn't wear a tag so much as test it. Slobber, water-bowl face-plants, sticky summer air and the sand they track in after a beach day they complained through the entire time. Printed tags fade and peel when they meet all that.

Ours don't, because the words are laser-cut into the metal. There's no paint on top to chip, wash or sweat off. On colored tags the laser lifts the anodized color and exposes bright silver underneath; on stainless and brass the mark goes dark and deep.

The joke stays readable long after the summer is over, which in Florida means never.

## Which shape fits your Frenchie

Tags here come in one size per shape, so picking a shape is picking a size. For a Frenchie, the sweet spot is the bone, heart or paw. All three are Medium, about 1.5 inches, which suits a standard collar and a medium neck without swallowing it. Want it a little bigger? The military runs about 2 inches and gives long sayings room to breathe. Want it smaller? The round is about 1.2 inches. The polished stainless bone is the biggest we make at 48 mm, and it comes with a paw cut-out, which is handy if your Frenchie also likes to be noticed. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.

## Frenchie tag FAQ

Still stuck? [hello@saltydogtags.com](mailto:hello@saltydogtags.com). A human answers. The dogs supervise.

**Which tag shape fits a Frenchie?**

Tags come in one size per shape, so picking a shape is picking a size. For a Frenchie, the bone, heart or paw (all Medium, about 1.5 inches) is the sweet spot. Want it bigger? The military is about 2 inches. Want it smaller? The round is about 1.2 inches.

**Will humidity, pool water or drool ruin an engraved frenchie tag?**

No. The words are laser-cut into the metal, not printed on top, so there's no ink to fade, peel or rinse off. Sweat, slobber and salt air don't erase an engraving.

**Can I put my frenchie's name and my phone number on the tag?**

Yes, that's the standard build. The funny saying goes on the front, and your dog's name plus your phone number are engraved on the back. A third back line is optional.

**Do you have sayings written specifically for French bulldogs?**

Nine of them on this page, about the bat ears, the screaming, the splooting, the gas and the hard no on midday walks. Every one fits the tag as written, and the full sayings list has hundreds more.

**What happens if the engraving comes out wrong?**

Custom tags aren't returnable, but if we mis-engrave it, we remake it free. Send a photo and we re-cut it. Tags are engraved and shipped from Lehi, Utah in 1 to 2 business days, then USPS takes 3 to 5 days in transit. US only.
