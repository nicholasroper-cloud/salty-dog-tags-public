## Built for a dog who is mostly zoomies and gas

Anyone who owns a Boston knows the routine. The tuxedo makes them look like they are headed to a wedding, and then they clear the room with one toot and fall asleep snoring on your pillow. That big round head leaves very little room for polite behavior.

A Boston never runs at one speed. It is nap, nap, nap, then thirty seconds of couch-surfing zoomies, then nap again. The tag takes all of that, so it gets laser engraved into the metal. Nothing to peel off when they skid into the coffee table.

Those bug eyes also mean a lot of face-first adventures, so the name and number go on the back where they stay readable.

## Which shape fits your Boston Terrier

Tags here come in one size per shape, so picking a shape is picking a size. For a Boston Terrier, the sweet spot is the bone, heart or paw. All three are Medium, about 1.5 inches, which suits a standard collar and a medium neck without swallowing it. Want it a little bigger? The military runs about 2 inches and gives long sayings room to breathe. Want it smaller? The round is about 1.2 inches. The polished stainless bone is the biggest we make at 48 mm, and it comes with a paw cut-out, which is handy if your Boston Terrier also likes to be noticed. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.

## Boston tag FAQ

Still stuck? [hello@saltydogtags.com](mailto:hello@saltydogtags.com). A human answers. The dogs supervise.

**Which tag shape fits a Boston Terrier?**

Tags come in one size per shape, so picking a shape is picking a size. For a Boston Terrier, the bone, heart or paw (all Medium, about 1.5 inches) is the sweet spot. Want it bigger? The military is about 2 inches. Want it smaller? The round is about 1.2 inches.

**Will mud, drool and rough play wear off the engraving?**

No. The text is laser engraved into the metal, so scuffing doesn't erase it.

**Can I put my Boston's name and my phone number on the tag?**

Yes. Saying on the front, name and phone number on the back.

**Do you have sayings written specifically for Bostons?**

12 on this page. Each fits the tag as written.

**What should I put on a Boston Terrier's tag?**

Pick one joke for the front and keep it under two short lines, like Tuxedo on. Manners off. Put your dog's name and a phone number on the back. Skip anything cutesy that hides the number.

**What happens if the engraving comes out wrong?**

If we mis-engrave it, we remake it free. Tags are engraved and shipped from Lehi, Utah in 1 to 2 business days, then USPS takes 3 to 5 days in transit. US only.
