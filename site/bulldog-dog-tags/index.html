Bulldogs are hard on a tag. This one can take it.

A bulldog does not wear a tag so much as marinate it. Drool, slobber, the water bowl they half-climb into, the dirt they plow with their face, the nap in direct sun, the snoring that rattles the whole collar for six hours a night. Printed tags give up. Engraved ones don't.

We cut the words into the metal with a fiber laser. Nothing is painted on, so there's nothing to chip or peel off. On the colored tags, the laser lifts the anodized color and exposes bright silver underneath, so the letters stay readable as the tag gets scratched up. On stainless and brass, the mark goes dark and deep.

Slobber-proof is just a side effect of using metal instead of ink.

Seven bulldogs, one laser, zero boring tags

We didn't start a tag company because we're crafty. We started it because we have seven English bulldogs in Lehi, Utah, they kept losing their tags, and the plain ones at the pet store cost more than a decent lunch. So we bought a laser and started making our own, with the jokes we were already making out loud.

Whisky's tag came first. Polished stainless, paw cut-out, four lines he did not approve. Everything on this site came after that one.

Which shape fits your Bulldog

Tags here come in one size per shape, so the shape you pick sets the size. A Bulldog has a big head and a thick collar, and a small tag disappears against all that. Go with the military, about 2 inches, or the polished stainless bone, the biggest we make at 48 mm, with a paw cut-out. Both are easy to read at a glance and easy to find under the fur. The bone, heart and paw are Medium, about 1.5 inches. They work if you like a quieter tag, or if the collar is slim. Leave the round for the lapdogs. Every shape gets the same engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.

Bulldog tag FAQ

Still stuck? hello@saltydogtags.com. A human answers. The dogs supervise.

Which tag shape fits a Bulldog?

Tags come in one size per shape, so the shape sets the size. A Bulldog has a big head and a thick collar, so go with the military (about 2 inches) or the polished stainless bone (48 mm, with a paw cut-out). The bone, heart and paw (about 1.5 inches) suit a slimmer collar. Leave the round for the lapdogs.

Will drool and slobber ruin an engraved bulldog tag?

No. The words are cut into the metal with a laser, not printed on top, so there's no ink to wash off. Anodized aluminum, stainless and brass all shrug off water.

Can I put my bulldog's name and my phone number on the tag?

Yes, that's the standard build. The funny saying goes on the front, and your dog's name plus your phone number are engraved on the back. A third back line is optional.

Do you have sayings written specifically for bulldogs?

Twelve of them on this page, about snoring, gas, wrinkles, the underbite, the loaf shape and the stubbornness. There's a full bulldog category in our sayings list too.

What happens if the engraving comes out wrong?

Custom tags aren't returnable, but if we mis-engrave it, we remake it free. Send a photo and we re-cut it. Tags are engraved and shipped from Lehi, Utah in 1 to 2 business days, then USPS takes 3 to 5 days in transit. US only.
