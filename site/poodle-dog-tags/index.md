## Which shape fits your Poodle

Tags here come in one size per shape, so the shape you pick is the size you get. Small collars and small necks want small tags. For a Poodle, start with the round, about 1.2 inches, or the heart, about 1.5 inches. Both sit flat and stay out of the way of a tiny chin. The bone and paw are also about 1.5 inches if you want the classic look. Skip the military, which is about 2 inches, and the polished bone, the biggest tag we make at 48 mm. They'll look like a dinner plate on a Poodle. Every shape gets the same two-sided engraving: saying on the front, name and number on the back. Pick the shape. The size comes with it.

Stainless and brass add $4, polished adds $6. The preview in the builder is exactly what we engrave.

## Poodle tag FAQ

Still stuck? [hello@saltydogtags.com](mailto:hello@saltydogtags.com). A human answers. The dogs supervise.

**Which tag shape fits a Poodle?**

Tags come in one size per shape, so the shape sets the size. For a Poodle, start with the round (about 1.2 inches) or the heart (about 1.5 inches). Both sit flat under a small chin. The military (about 2 inches) and the polished stainless bone (48 mm) are too big for a dog this size.

**Will baths, pools and humidity ruin an engraved tag?**

No. The words are laser engraved into the metal, not printed on the surface, so there's no ink to wash off or fade.

**Can I put my poodle's name and my phone number on the tag?**

Yes, that's the standard build. The funny saying goes on the front, and your dog's name and your phone number are engraved on the back.

**Do you have sayings written specifically for poodles?**

Eight on this page, covering the brains, the haircuts, the "hypoallergenic" label and the French-but-actually-German thing. Or write your own in the builder.

**What happens if the engraving comes out wrong?**

If we mis-engrave it, we remake it free. Tags are engraved and shipped from Lehi, Utah in 1 to 2 business days, then USPS takes 3 to 5 days in transit. US only.
